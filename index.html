Your memories.
Your photo library.

Move your own Snapchat Memories into the MemoryMover album on your device, with their dates, available GPS location and supported photo caption overlays.

Your export is processed on your device. No MemoryMover account, developer upload server, developer analytics, ads or tracking. Store services handle purchases and their own diagnostics.

From your export to your album

  1. Request your export from Snapchat. Use “Download My Data” and include your Memories.
  2. Choose the export in MemoryMover. Select your ZIP files or an extracted export folder. Parsing and media preparation happen locally.
  3. Save to your photo library. Files already in the export stay local; missing media is downloaded directly from supported Snapchat HTTPS links in your export.

Dates and location depend on the information available in the export or supported source files. Separate overlays on videos are not supported; the app reports unsupported or ambiguous items instead of silently saving an incomplete version.

The first 200 unique saves are free. Unlimited is an optional one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple or Google. See purchase and restore help.
